Added MP4, M4V, MOV and WebM subtitle import, drag-and-drop and folder scanning, with bundled timed-text conversion to SRT and timing-offset tests. Fixed rejected subagent starts aborting other workers and misleading missing-component errors; preserve valid results and repair missing entries with bounded retries, while quota and authentication errors still pause the job.
